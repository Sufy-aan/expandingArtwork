How it works

 

Every square starts blank. Click one to select it, or click-and-drag to select a whole block at once. Selected squares go into your cart at the bottom of the screen — check out once to buy them all together for $1 each, with one color and caption applied to the batch. The moment every square on the board is sold, the whole canvas grows ×10 and stays exactly as it was.

This is a working demo — balances are play money tracked in this browser. No real charges happen anywhere on this page.
